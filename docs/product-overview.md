# Product overview

## Purpose

engplatform2 helps mid-size engineering and construction firms replace fragmented Excel, WhatsApp, and paper reporting with a dependable project-management platform.

## MVP users

- **Site Engineer:** submit daily reports and raise variations from site.
- **Project Manager:** monitor project activity, issues, and variation decisions.
- **Quantity Surveyor:** maintain BOQs, review variations, and prepare valuations.
- **Director:** view the company portfolio, progress, and financial exposure.

Clients will receive read-only project access in a later phase.

## First milestone

The initial usable release will cover secure sign-in, project and BOQ setup, offline-capable daily reports, and a web view of submitted reports and progress.
